## ✅ Resolved Cooldown Bypass Issue
We identified a vulnerability that allowed players to bypass the cooldown mechanism associated with the water button. This issue posed a risk to the intended pacing and mechanics of the game. 

To mitigate this problem and ensure a fair gameplay experience for all players, we have temporarily disabled the quit button as a precautionary measure. This will prevent further exploitation of the issue while we work on implementing a more effective and long-term solution.

Our development team is actively addressing the situation to provide a clean and permanent fix in the near future.
